# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, and wherever
someone represents the project. It covers people and the AI agents that work on their behalf alike.

In short:
- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Disagree about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks or discrimination.
- Don't publish anyone's private information.
- Keep to the facts. Growers, neighbours, TTB staff and commenters on the docket are discussed with courtesy, here and
  everywhere else.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer at **district@hickorysandsdistrictava.org**. Every report is reviewed and kept
confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block anyone who breaks
this code, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
