The petitioners

Three growers.
One petition.

Dan McLaughlin of Robert Clay Vineyards, William R. Parr, Jr. of Parr Vineyards and Parr Cellars, and Drew Tallent of Tallent Vineyards petitioned TTB to establish Hickory Sands District.

The work

Who wrote
and drew it.

Tesha Parr Solomon, of Parr Cellars and Parr Vineyards, coordinated the petition and wrote it. Dan McLaughlin wrote the boundary narrative, drew the boundary, and did all the spatial work for the GIS layers.

This site

Independent
of TTB.

The official record of the proposal is TTB’s: Notice No. 243 and the public docket. This site points to it and does not speak for the Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau.

Privacy

What the
form keeps.

The vineyard form on The vineyards posts a vineyard’s name, county, acres, varieties, year and blocks (or the place of a dropped pin) publicly, on GitHub. The sender’s name, phone and email go to a private table that only the petitioners read, and are deleted a year after the submission is closed. The form uses Esri’s maps and search, and Supabase.
