The line

The boundary shown is the petitioners’ proposed revision of October 15, 2026, drawn from TTB’s published description on the USGS 1:24,000 historical quadrangles. On this site it is simplified by 12 meters for display; the full geometry is in the public GIS files.

The vineyard form

“Add your vineyard” on The vineyards draws on Esri’s World Imagery and searches with Esri’s geocoding service. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person is sending. What is sent goes to a function hosted by Supabase, which keeps the sender’s name, phone and email in a private table and posts the vineyard’s public details as an issue on GitHub.
