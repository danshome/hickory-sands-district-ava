The sandstone was laid down in a Cambrian sea, roughly half a billion years ago. The proposed district follows the Hickory Sandstone Member formation: deep, sandy, well-drained ground over the Hickory Aquifer.

The line is drawn to leave the granite of the surrounding Llano Uplift outside.
