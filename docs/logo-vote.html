      Send in a design

      A photo of a sketch is fine. Hand drawn is welcome.

      From your photos, or take one now. A JPEG or PNG works best.

      We don't ask for your name and none is shown. Each design is checked by the petitioners before it shows here.

      How the vote works
      • Each design comes around once on your phone. Your votes are counted without your name; this phone remembers which designs you've voted on.
      • New designs are added as growers send them in.
      • Once a design has at least votes and fewer than of them are likes, the petitioners may retire it. Retired designs leave the vote and are kept below for the record.
      • When the vote closes, the petitioners pick the mark from the results, and it is shown at the top of this page.

      Questions? Email district@hickorysandsdistrictava.org.
