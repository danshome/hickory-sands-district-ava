Hickory Sands District is a proposed American Viticultural Area. It becomes one only if TTB establishes it in a final rule.

  1. February 2023

    The petition is filed

    Dan McLaughlin of Robert Clay Vineyards, William R. Parr, Jr. of Parr Vineyards and Parr Cellars, and Drew Tallent of Tallent Vineyards petition TTB to establish the district.

  2. June 6, 2023

    The petition, as amended

    The petitioners amend the petition, and the proposed name becomes Hickory Sands District. This is the version of the petition in the public docket.

  3. August 14, 2026

    TTB publishes the proposal

    Notice No. 243, 91 FR 52576, proposes the Llano Uplift and the Hickory Sands District viticultural areas.

  4. October 13, 2026

    The comment period closes

    The comments, a petitioner’s among them, are in the public docket, TTB-2026-0005.

  5. October 15, 2026

    The proposed revision

    After discussing the boundary with TTB, the petitioners propose changes to its description and draw the line on the USGS survey maps: 193.83 square miles in 104 steps.

  6. Pending

    TTB’s decision

    TTB considers the comments and decides whether to establish the viticultural area.
