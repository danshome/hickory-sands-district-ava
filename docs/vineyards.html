Add your
vineyard.

If you grow grapes in the proposed Hickory Sands District, or elsewhere in the proposed Llano Uplift AVA, add your vineyard here. It goes to the petitioners for the proposed Hickory Sands District AVA, who keep this register. It takes a few minutes on your phone.

  1. Find it on the map
  2. Tap the corners of each block
  3. Tell us about it
  4. Leave a phone number or email
  5. Check it and send

Your answers so far are kept on this device, apart from your name, phone and email.

Need a hand? Email district@hickorysandsdistrictava.org.
