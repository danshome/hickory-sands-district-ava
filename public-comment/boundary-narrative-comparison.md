# Boundary narrative comparison: petition vs. Notice No. 243

Prepared October 13, 2026, by Dan McLaughlin, co-petitioner, Hickory Sands District AVA. Attachment 1 to my comment on Docket No. TTB-2026-0005.

Sources: the Hickory Sands District AVA petition as amended June 7, 2023 ("Written Boundary Description," 102 steps), and Notice No. 243, 91 FR 52576 (August 14, 2026), proposed 27 CFR 9.__, "Hickory Sands District," paragraph (c) (96 steps). This is a summary of the differences, not a complete step-by-step crosswalk. Petition step N corresponds to proposed step N-1 through proposed step 88, because the notice merges the petition's beginning point into its step 1.

## Structural (no geographic change)

- The notice merges petition steps 1 and 2 into its step 1.
- The notice adds USGS quadrangle map-crossing notes in 22 steps.
- Minor wording normalization ("marked" added to elevation points, "contour line" to "elevation contour," etc.).

The petition route stays north of the granite around Grit, along State Highway 29. The proposed route runs south of it, along Honey Creek and U.S. Highway 377, and adds a section of approximately 4.32 square miles (2,767 acres) of exposed granite to the AVA. The requested change is to keep proposed step 89 and replace proposed steps 90 through 92 with petition steps 91 through 98.

## Lane qualifiers

- Petition steps 11 and 12 route the boundary along the "westbound lane" of State Highway 29 and U.S. Highway 377; proposed steps 10 and 11 omit the lane.
- Petition steps 92 and 93 route it along the "eastbound lane" of State Highway 29; these steps were removed with the western rewrite.
- Together, the lane routing takes the boundary out along one side of the highways and back along the other, excluding the granite between them.

## Other differences

1. **Beginning point:** petition "just east of Bench Mark 1678"; proposed "just west of benchmark 1,678." The 1,748-foot elevation point on U.S. Highway 377 is retained in both; the point lies west of the benchmark on the map.
2. **Petition step 28 / proposed step 27:** "west-northwest" became "northwest" along Old Katemcy Mason Road. Both follow the named road; no change to the path.
3. **Petition step 83 / proposed step 82:** the petition ends the 1,500-foot contour run "to the 1511-foot elevation point at its intersection with ... Ranch Road 1723"; the proposed text ends it "to a point due east of the 1,511-foot elevation point." I accept the proposed wording.

## Wording corrections requested in the proposed text (several carry over from the petition)

- **Step 4:** "the marked 1,866-foot elevation point." The Long Mountain map prints both BM 1866 (the intended point) and a separate spot elevation 1866 about 1.2 km east. The step should name "benchmark 1,866."
- **Steps 90 and 91 (proposed):** "State Highway 377" should be "U.S. Highway 377."
- **Step 44 (proposed):** "benchmark 1,495"; the map prints BR 1495, a bridge elevation.
- **Step 49 (proposed):** "1,586-elevation point" is missing "foot."
- **Step 85 (proposed):** "1,801-foot summit"; the map prints 1802.
- **Step 84 (proposed):** "1,610-foot summit"; the 1610 mark is printed in a drainage.
- **Steps 80 and 81 (proposed):** the 1967 Mason map prints no road south of State Highway 29 at its intersection with Old Pontotoc Road and Copperhead Road, only a land line.
- **Step 46 (proposed):** Private Roads 626 and 669 join the east-west road at two junctions about 54 m apart, not one intersection.

## Area check (GRS 1980 ellipsoid, NAD83)

| Boundary | Area |

The notice describes a 193-square-mile AVA. The difference between the proposed text and the petition routing is essentially the Grit section; the remaining differences along the lane routing and near the 1765 summit are small and partly offsetting. Separately, the published polygon departs from the proposed text in 69 places totaling about 790 acres (see the comment, Section VI).

Well data: Texas Water Development Board Groundwater Database, Mason County irrigation-well records; no wells within the Grit section, nearest recorded well about 0.15 miles from its edge.
